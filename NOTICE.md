## The games

The games Legendary reads and plays are copyrighted works of their publishers and developers. They are not part of Legendary's own work, do not fall under the GNU General Public License, and are not included in this repository: Legendary works on copies you have.

They were made by Legend Entertainment between 1990 and 1995, and the rights to them have since gone separate ways; there is no one holder for all of them. As of November 2026:

- **Timequest, the Spellcasting games, Eric the Unready and Mission Critical** are sold by Ziggurat Interactive, on Steam and GOG among others. Ziggurat also holds the Legend Entertainment name and logo as trademarks.
- **Gateway, Gateway II: Homeworld and Companions of Xanth** aren't sold anywhere. Each was made from a novel, so besides the rights to the game there are the rights to the book: Frederik Pohl's, through his estate, for the two *Gateway* games, and Piers Anthony's for *Xanth*.

This is a summary of what the stores and publishers show, not legal advice. Anyone wanting to distribute a game should ask its rights holders.
